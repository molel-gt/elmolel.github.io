Research

My research is on mathematical modeling of electrochemical systems such as batteries. Specifically, I study the impact of geometry and chemical properties on reaction distribution to optimize these systems for rate capability and reduced susceptibility to degradation. I build reduced-order and microstructure-resolved models using tools such as complex analysis and finite element analysis.
